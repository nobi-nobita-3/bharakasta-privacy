## 1. About the Game

Bharakasta – Village Edition is a traditional board game offering offline gameplay, online multiplayer, and optional voice communication between players.

This policy explains how information is used when playing the game.

## 2. Information Used

The game may process the following information:

- **Anonymous player identity:** Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous identifier to support online gameplay.
- **Game information:** Player display names, room codes, multiplayer sessions, game moves, and match-related information.
- **Safety information:** Player reports and blocking preferences used to support player safety.
- **Voice communication:** When the microphone is enabled, audio is processed to provide real-time voice chat with other players.
- **Technical information:** Firebase and other relevant service providers may process technical, device, and network information required to operate their services.

## 3. Microphone and Voice Chat

Microphone access is used for the game's optional voice chat feature.

Voice communication is transmitted using real-time communication technology. Other players participating in the voice session can hear transmitted audio.

Players can turn off their microphone using the available game controls.

## 4. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide multiplayer gameplay, connect players, maintain game sessions, support voice communication, process player reports, prevent abuse, and maintain the service.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The game uses Google Firebase services for anonymous authentication and online multiplayer functionality.

If in-app cosmetic purchases are enabled, purchases will be processed through Google Play Billing. The game uses purchase status information to manage cosmetic unlocks.

Relevant third-party privacy policies include:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## 6. Information Shared With Other Players

Other participants in an online room may see player display names, game actions, and match information.

When voice chat is enabled, participating players can hear voice audio transmitted during the session.
